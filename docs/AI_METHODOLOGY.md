# AI Methodology & Grounding Architecture

JanSahayakAI uses a **hybrid AI-deterministic architecture** to provide reliable, factually grounded answers to Indian citizens inquiring about government welfare schemes.

---

## 1. Core Principle: AI Does Not Compute Eligibility

A fundamental vulnerability of naive LLM implementations in public administration is **hallucination of eligibility rules** (e.g. telling an ineligible citizen they qualify, or inventing benefits that do not exist).

In JanSahayakAI:
1. **Deterministic Eligibility Engine (`core/eligibility.py`) is the sole computational authority.**
2. The AI service (`ai/service.py`) **only acts as an explanation and conversational layer**, never the decider.
3. Before generating any response, the deterministic engine runs first with the citizen's profile and provides structured fact tokens (`ELIGIBLE`, `INELIGIBLE`, `UNKNOWN`, and reason lists).

---

## 2. Scheme Catalog Grounding

The AI is strictly grounded in `schemes.json` (the 25 verified central and state government schemes):

1. **System Prompt Injection:** Only verified scheme facts from `schemes.json` are injected into the LLM system prompt.
2. **Context Sanitization:** Raw citizen inputs are scrubbed of PII (Aadhaar 12-digit patterns are masked to `XXXX-XXXX-1234`, PAN patterns to `XXXXX1234X`, and street addresses/raw file payloads are stripped).
3. **Structured Context:** Deterministic evaluation output is passed directly to the LLM context so the model cannot contradict the programmatic result.

---

## 3. Post-Generation Output Validation (`core/ai_validator.py`)

Every AI response generated by the external LLM provider passes through an automated validation layer before being returned to the citizen:

1. **Contradiction Detection:**
   - If the deterministic engine marks a scheme as `INELIGIBLE`, the validator scans the AI response. If the AI incorrectly asserts "you are eligible" or "you qualify", the response is flagged.
   - If flagged, the response is replaced with a safe deterministic fallback message stating the exact eligibility criteria that were not satisfied.
2. **Fabrication Defense:**
   - If the AI mentions schemes outside the authorized catalog, or promises guaranteed disbursement, the validator intervenes.

---

## 4. Tri-State Status & `UNKNOWN` Handling

Government schemes frequently depend on criteria that cannot be determined from basic demographic surveys (e.g. land records, caste sub-classifications, enrollment in secondary registries).

- **`ELIGIBLE`**: All required conditions match the citizen profile.
- **`INELIGIBLE`**: One or more disqualifying conditions are definitively violated (e.g. age out of bounds, state mismatch, exclusion criteria met).
- **`UNKNOWN`**: Crucial information is missing or cannot be verified without manual document inspection.

When a status is `UNKNOWN`, the AI:
- Explicitly states: *"Eligibility cannot be definitively confirmed based on the available information."*
- Lists the specific missing documents or criteria needed.
- Provides the official government portal link for verified enrollment.

---

## 5. Hallucination Limitations

While the deterministic grounding and validation layers significantly reduce hallucination:
- LLM phrasing may occasionally use imperfect nuance in regional languages.
- Government rules are updated periodically by ministries; local policy updates must be refreshed in `schemes.json`.
- The system explicitly advises citizens to confirm final approval on official portals (e.g., `pmkisan.gov.in`, `pmjay.gov.in`).
